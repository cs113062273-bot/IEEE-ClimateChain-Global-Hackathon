| Official criterion | GridProof evidence to foreground |

| Innovation & Creativity | Energy decisions, physical constraints and independent source-aware verification in one workflow. |
| Technical Execution | Real forecasting, real MILP execution, deliberate tampering rejection and actual registry reads. |

**No numerical weights are published in the inspected Overview or Rules.** Do not assume equal 20% weights. No statistical threshold is added there. An operational AI forecast can support these criteria without a claim that it beats every simpler predictor.
