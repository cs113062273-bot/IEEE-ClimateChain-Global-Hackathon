# GridProof — Trustworthy Carbon-Aware Microgrid Orchestration

**Primary track:** Renewable Energy & Energy Trading.

GridProof helps a campus or fleet operator choose when flexible electricity demand should run and makes the resulting estimate independently inspectable. Public national GB grid data and London weather underpin a transparently simulated microgrid with rooftop PV, battery storage, EV deadlines and a contiguous lab workload.

A physically constrained mixed-integer optimizer compares immediate, cost-first, carbon-first and hybrid dispatch under the same workload. A chronological forecasting and evaluation pipeline prevents future actual observations from entering decision predictors. Retrospective outcomes score modeled location-based electricity emissions exposure; illustrative tariffs expose cost tradeoffs without pretending to be actual bills.

An evidence bundle records inputs, provenance fingerprints, methods, constraints and computed results. Independent canonical SHA256 verification detects changes. A Solidity registry running on a real local EVM chain prevents duplicate active registrations for the same site, exact interval and category, including variants that change model or scenario labels. Authorized revocation and replacement retain an auditable history.

The four-page dashboard connects source inspection, optimization, numerical replay and proof verification. An attributed offline sample supports reproducible demonstrations without provider network access. Acquisition scripts, source licences, test commands, experimental reports and limitations are included in the repository.

This prototype does not claim measured facility deployment, certified physical avoided emissions or tradable carbon credits. A blockchain commitment authenticates report integrity after registration, not source or meter truth. Commercial rollout would require compatible data access, point-in-time forecasts, validated meters and operational testing.
